# Expectation Must End In A Matcher

Title: fixed
Family: testing

Example source: development

## Explanation

`expect($value)` alone checks nothing. An expectation is a chain that ends in a call to a matcher, such as `toEqual`, `toBeNull`, `toBeTrue` or `toContain`, with at most one `->not` before it.

This error means the chain has no matcher at its end, the matcher is reached through `?->`, or something other than `->not` stands between `expect(...)` and the matcher.

## Fix

End the chain with the matcher that states what you expect: `expect($total)->toEqual(2);`. Call the matcher with `->`.

To negate, write `->not` without parentheses directly before the matcher: `expect($total)->not->toEqual(3);`.

## Example

```doria
use Doria\Std\Test\{it, expect};

it("adds two numbers", function (): void {
    expect(1 + 1);
});
```
